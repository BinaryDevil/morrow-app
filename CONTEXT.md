# Morrow

Morrow is a place-based experience-sharing context. It connects an account's local discoveries with the people who follow that account.

## Language

**Experience**:
A place-linked post with contextual tags, a visibility choice, and either a photo or caption.
_Avoid_: Post, review

**Follower**:
An account that has chosen to follow another account and can receive that account's follower-visible experiences.
_Avoid_: Friend, contact

**Follower-visible experience**:
An experience readable by its author and the author's followers.
_Avoid_: Public experience

**Private experience**:
An experience readable only by its author.
_Avoid_: Hidden experience
